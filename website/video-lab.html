UNDER THE HOOD

What each layer adds.

Read the exact contracts ↗
01 / PIXELS → BOXES

Detection is a snapshot.

The adapter resizes and normalizes a frame for its model. It converts predictions to person boxes and filters them at 0.30 confidence. A box does not contain an action label.

02 / BOXES → TEMPORARY IDS

Matching connects samples.

The tracker matches overlapping boxes between samples and waits for two matches before confirming an ID. It does not understand identity or recover it reliably behind a shelf.

03 / IDS + TIME → RULE OUTPUT

A timer needs evidence.

The engine checks the bottom-center anchor, confidence and successive observations. Three supported seconds produce one dwell event. Missing evidence resets that episode.
